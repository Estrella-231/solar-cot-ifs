# Train-only historical COT input-origin diagnostic

## Result

Across **20,206 training AGRI frames** on 220 BJT dates, the existing full-R in-sample bank and date-held-out fold-R inference retain a similar broad spatial pattern but differ in local amplitude. On the common daytime support where both outputs lie in [0,100], there are **2,545,664 pixel pairs**. The pixel-weighted map disagreement is **MAE 2.926 COT** and **RMSE 5.968 COT**, with pixel correlation 0.942. Median absolute difference is 0.667; the 95th percentile is 13.246.

On that same common support, full-R versus date-held-out R output means are **12.632 vs 11.256 COT** (OOF minus full = -1.376, or -10.9% of the full-R mean). Their p90 values are 37.02 vs 32.67; p99 values are 69.29 vs 53.33. The outputs are correlated, yet the held-out fold bank has a lower mean and lighter upper tail on this restricted support.

## Important out-of-range behavior

The common-support scores exclude pixels outside [0,100] by design and do not clip them. On daytime pixels, the full-R bank has 6,970 outputs above 100 (0.273%), with maximum **449380.2**; the fold-OOF bank has 2,564 above 100 (0.100%), with maximum **12648.7**. Both have no below-zero daytime pixels. The sparse extreme tail materially inflates raw means and standard deviations, so the figure's daily means and discrepancy RMSE use only the shared [0,100] support. The out-of-range tail remains reported separately and is not treated as physically valid COT.

## Interpretation

This establishes a plausible input-source mismatch: full-R train inputs were generated by a model that had seen those dates; the comparison uses an R checkpoint whose training excluded each date. The fold-OOF output is on average lower and less dispersed on common in-range pixels, while spatial patterns remain strongly correlated. A downstream GHI head could therefore see different COT feature distributions between fitting and held-out use. This experiment does **not** tell us which output is closer to physical COT, and it does **not** establish COT forecast skill or explain a GHI gain/loss. CPP/COT reference targets and GHI labels were not used.

## Scope and audit correction

All five frozen R checkpoints inferred only their assigned training-date frames. The five fold shards contain {'0': 4174, '1': 4052, '2': 4008, '3': 4031, '4': 3941}; their unique train keys sum to 20,206. No validation AGRI frames were inferred. Test, buffer, CPP/QC and GHI label payload reads are recorded as zero.

The original automated post-inference auditor performed a global finite-value scan and whole-file hash on the existing train/validation COT bank. The first local train-only revision (`audit_oof_history_bank_train_only.py`) removed the global scan but still hashed the whole prediction file. Both therefore touched validation **model-output values** during technical checks; neither inferred validation AGRI, read validation labels, used validation values in metrics, or selected a model. The accepted v3 audit re-ran the comparison without numerically scanning or hashing validation predictions. It reads full-bank row metadata to map keys but accesses prediction values only for the 20,206 train keys; the reported bank-value hash covers those selected train rows. This deviation and correction are retained explicitly.

## Next step

Do not train or claim GHI benefit from this diagnostic alone. The full-region CPP source gate remains blocked, and this comparison does not identify which COT bank is more physically accurate. Any GHI follow-up requires a separately specified paired training-input comparison and an unexposed evaluation cohort.
